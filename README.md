**Hi there! :wave:** 

👨‍🎓 I am Pierre Nassivera, a PhD Candidate at Laval University in Québec, Canada.  

🪶 My PhD research focuses on the Bicknell's Thrush (_Catharus bicknelli_), a Vulnerable songbird that breeds in the high-elevation balsam fir forests of northeastern North America (https://www.iucnredlist.org/species/22728467/180783383). More specifically, I aim to develop a high-resolution habitat model for Bicknell’s Thrush across the Quebec region and apply this model to support conservation planning and decision-making in response to a range of anthropogenic and natural threats.

🏔️ Beyond my PhD research, my broader interests include both theoretical and applied aspects of ecology, evolution, and conservation of birds inhabiting cold ecosystems, including Arctic, sub-Arctic, boreal, and alpine environments.
